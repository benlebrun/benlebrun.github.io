I’m currently a Master’s student in Linguistics at McGill University and <a href="https://mila.quebec/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Mila</a> supervised by Tim O’Donnell. I am generally interested in computational cognitive science and probabilistic generative models. I work on computational approaches to language grounding, with a particular interest in computational pragmatics.

I recently completed an internship at Mila, and graduated in Computer Science and Linguistics at McGill. During that time, I worked at <a href="http://mcqll.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">MCQLL</a>, the Montreal Computational and Quantitative Linguistics Lab, where I studied [whether neural language models accurately approximate rare sentences](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=bTteFbU99ye). I was also a research assistant at <a href="https://txtlab.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">txtLab</a>, a laboratory for cultural analytics, where I used computational models to study [corporate acquisition in local news](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/14614448221079030) and [playlist curation on Spotify](https://txtlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Spotify2020.pdf).

You can find a list of my publications on [my Google Scholar page](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=QTIN18wAAAAJ&hl=en).
