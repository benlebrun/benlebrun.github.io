I’m currently a Master’s student in Linguistics at McGill University and <a href="https://mila.quebec/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Mila</a> supervised by Tim O’Donnell. I work on computational approaches to language grounding, with a particular interest in computational pragmatics.

I recently completed an internship at Mila, and graduated in Computer Science and Linguistics at McGill. During that time, I worked at <a href="http://mcqll.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">MCQLL</a>, the Montreal Computational and Quantitative Linguistics Lab, where I studied <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformer_(machine_learning_model)" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Transformer-based language models</a>. I was also a research assistant at <a href="https://txtlab.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">txtLab</a>, a laboratory for cultural analytics, where I used computational models to study corporate acquisition in local news and playlist curation on Spotify.
